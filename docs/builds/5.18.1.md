* **Summary:** Fix the floor-plan canvas view jumping across the screen after a pin drag. The Stage's `onDragEnd` was running on every `dragend`, including the ones that bubble up from the PhotoPin's own draggable Group; in that case `e.target` is the pin (not the Stage), and `e.target.x()/y()` are the pin's plan-pixel coordinates — applying those as the Stage's pan offset sent the view far off-screen (especially noticeable at high zoom, where the canvas appeared completely blank after a drag). Guard the Stage handler so it only updates `stagePos` when the Stage itself is the drag source. Pin-drag persistence still works as before (PhotoPin's own `onDragEnd` is unchanged). Web-only; no iOS, server, or schema change.
