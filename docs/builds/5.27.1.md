* **Summary:** Two web-side floor-plan changes to match iOS behavior more closely.
  **(1) Black band replaces the orange `+N` badge** as the "more than one photo here" indicator. Same visual iOS uses (`PlanViewerView.swift::bubble(for:radius:)` — `strokeBorder(Color.black, lineWidth: max(2, radius * 0.18))`). The band's PRESENCE signals "multiple photos at this location" without claiming a specific count — fixes the Build #5.26.1 bug where the badge override on a spatial-cluster representative was displacing the iOS-group-size count, leading to inaccurate numbers (a group-of-13 lead showing only the cluster-of-3 count, etc.). Now both group leads AND spatial cluster reps render the same band; no count is shown on the bubble. Heading arrow is also suppressed on cluster reps (members can have wildly different bearings).
  **(2) New `PhotoPreviewPanel` replaces `PhotoLightbox` on the plan page.** Docks to the RIGHT side of the viewport when width ≥ 1024 px (so the plan stays in view at ~55% of the screen), otherwise BOTTOM. Includes a `Group / All on plan` nav-scope toggle mirroring iOS's `PreviewNavMode`. Arrow keys + on-screen prev/next iterate within the active scope. Cluster bubble click → opens panel with the cluster's expanded membership (cluster members + their iOS-group siblings de-duped) as the "Group" scope, starting at the lowest-sequence member. Singleton group lead click → opens panel with that photo's iOS group as the scope. PhotoLightbox stays on the project-detail-page grid (no plan to keep in view there). Web-only; no iOS, server, shared, or schema change.
