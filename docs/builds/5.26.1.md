* **Summary:** Two related floor-plan canvas features.
  **(1) Adjustable pin size.** New +/- toolbar control next to the Zoom indicator, range 50% — 250%, step 10%, session-only (no persistence per user request). Every pin's visual footprint scales uniformly: circle radius, badge, sequence text, heading-arrow length and stroke. Plan-pixel coordinates are unaffected — only render geometry changes.
  **(2) Spatial conflict avoidance** mirroring iOS `ClusterFanning.detectPrimaryClusters`. New `web/src/lib/clusterFanning.ts` is a 1:1 TS port of the iOS union-find algorithm. After the existing group-collapse filter, primary pins whose centers fall within one bubble-radius of each other on the plan get clustered; each cluster renders as a single representative bubble at the cluster centroid with a `+N` badge for the other pins it stands for. Click → small floating popover lists each underlying photo as a `#seq` chip; click a chip → opens the lightbox at that photo. Cluster collision radius is derived from `bubbleScale × baseFitScale` so it tracks the pin-size slider but is independent of wheel zoom (cluster at zoom=1 stays a cluster at zoom=4). Clustering is suppressed when pin-drag mode is on, so dragging operates on individual pins. Web-only; no iOS, server, shared, or schema change.
