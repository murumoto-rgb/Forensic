* **Summary:** Two-part fix for "Sync now does nothing visible" on Build #5.10.1. (1) `PhotoSyncer.syncProject` iterates plans before photos so floor plan backgrounds appear within seconds even on a fresh install. (2) New `preCheckUploadedPhotos` step uses the existing `/v1/sync/files/check` endpoint to bulk-mark photos already in R2 as uploaded in the local tracker, in chunks of 500. Previously, a fresh install with 1000s of photos would redundantly re-upload every one of them via per-photo round-trips before plans were even reached — now the bulk check turns that into a handful of DB queries, marks them in the tracker, and the per-photo loop short-circuits silently. iOS-only; no schema or web change.
