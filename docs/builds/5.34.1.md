* **Summary:** Fix floor-plan recenter math so the selected pin lands at the centre of the canvas's CURRENTLY-VISIBLE region, not at the centre of the Stage in Stage-local coords. Earlier passes used `(stageWidth - reserveRight) / 2` / `(stageHeight - reserveBottom) / 2`, which targets the Stage's geometric middle — fine when the Stage fits inside the viewport, off-screen-low when the Stage is taller than the viewport (typical landscape browser + portrait-ish plan). The recenter now reads `getBoundingClientRect()` for the canvas, intersects with the viewport (`window.innerWidth/Height`), subtracts the docked preview panel from whichever side it lives on, and converts the visible-region centroid into Stage-local coordinates for the pan calculation. Degenerate cases (canvas entirely scrolled off, or panel covers everything) bail rather than dividing by zero. Web-only; no shared/iOS/server change.
