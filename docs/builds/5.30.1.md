* **Summary:** Fix for Build #5.28.1: the canvas recenter was silently skipping non-primary group members (the "reshoots" surfaced via the Group nav scope in the preview panel). Only the group's PRIMARY photo has `planPixelX/Y` in the manifest — non-primaries share the primary's pin on the plan but don't carry their own coords. My recenter effect bailed out via `if (planPixelX == null) return;`, leaving the view stuck on whatever the previous recenter target was. The user saw this most clearly on a wide window when navigating within a group: the view stayed at the previously-selected primary's location while the preview showed an unrelated reshoot. Fix: when the previewed photo lacks its own coords, fall back to the group primary's coords for the recenter anchor. Web-only; no iOS, server, shared, or schema change.
