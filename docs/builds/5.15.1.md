* **Summary:** Web floor-plan / lightbox UX bundle. (1) Pin-drag save retries once on a network-level failure (Render cold-start window) and shows a human message + re-stash instead of Safari's opaque "Load failed". (2) Cursor shows "move" over a draggable pin, "zoom-in" over a click-only pin, "grab" on the canvas. (3) Lightbox preloads ±2 neighbours (URL + image bytes) for instant paging. (4) **Pins are locked by default** — a "🔒 Unlock pins" toggle in the toolbar must be clicked before any pin can be dragged, preventing accidental repositioning while panning/browsing. (5) After a drag completes, a confirm dialog ("Move photo #N to the new position?") gates the save; Cancel snaps the pin back to its pre-drag spot. Web-only; no iOS, server, or schema change.
